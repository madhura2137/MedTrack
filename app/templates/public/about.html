Project Mission

About MedTrack

A centralized, cloud-enabled healthcare management ecosystem engineered to bridge patients and healthcare practitioners through digital coordination.

The Problem

Traditional healthcare interactions are frequently fragmented: appointment confirmations occur via ad-hoc calls, medical histories are paper-bound, and diagnosis reports or prescriptions are easily misplaced. Patients experience anxiety over delays, while physicians lack instantaneous access to patient health logs.

The MedTrack Solution

MedTrack establishes a single source of truth. Patients book real-time appointments and review their diagnoses. Doctors review structured patient histories, manage daily consult queues, and generate clear digital prescriptions that notify the patient instantly.

Empowering Both Sides of Care

For Patients
Patient-centric control
  • Browse verified doctors by medical specialty and credentials.
  • Book, reschedule, and track appointment statuses in real time.
  • Secure digital storage for pre-existing conditions, allergies, and blood group.
  • Access doctor diagnosis reports with dosage and prescription logs anytime.
For Doctors
Clinical workflow optimization
  • Daily appointment agenda organized with live patient status tags.
  • Review authorized patient medical profiles prior to consultation.
  • Submit structured clinical diagnoses, medications, and dosage instructions.
  • Instant trigger of notifications to patient upon report finalization.
Cloud Roadmap

AWS Cloud Architecture

MedTrack is designed to progressively leverage enterprise AWS services for maximum uptime, data durability, and HIPAA-conscious security.

Amazon EC2 & Nginx

High-availability application tier hosting the Flask Gunicorn WSGI engine.

Amazon DynamoDB

NoSQL multi-region database storing user profiles, appointments, and diagnostic logs.

Amazon SNS

Push notification pipeline alerting patients of booking confirmations and prescriptions.

AWS IAM & VPC

Least-privilege role policies and isolated virtual network subnets.

Amazon CloudWatch

Real-time telemetry, server logs, latency tracking, and operational alerting.

Amazon Route 53

Global DNS routing providing reliable, low-latency domain name resolution.
